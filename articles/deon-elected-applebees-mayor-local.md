title: Deon Inaugurated As Mayor Of Local Applebee’s Grill + Bar
date: 2026-10-15 15:17
category: Local
author: Garrett Thistledown
dateline: OVERLAND PARK, KS
summary: After a grueling campaign centered on the revitalization of the happy hour menu, local resident Deon has taken the oath of office at his neighborhood chain restaurant.
---
Deon was officially sworn in as the Mayor of the Applebee’s Grill + Bar on Monday afternoon following a decisive landslide victory among the weekday happy hour regulars. The ceremony, held in a high-traffic corner booth near the bar, reportedly solidified Deon’s authority over the establishment’s 4,200 square feet of casual dining space.

The new mayor secured 84 percent of the vote after promising a bold Neighborhood First policy that prioritizes the timely delivery of boneless wings. According to franchise records, Deon’s frequent attendance and mastery of the menu options made him the natural choice for a constituency primarily composed of shift workers and people avoiding their families.

## A Mandate For Mozzarella

Institutional observers noted that Deon’s platform resonated with voters who felt the previous administration had been too conservative with the ranch dressing portions. Under his leadership, the municipality expects to see a 15 percent increase in the consumption of Dollaritas and a complete overhaul of the current jukebox queue.

> "His commitment to the Neighborhood is unmatched, provided the neighborhood is inside these four walls and smells faintly of charbroiled steak."

Mayor Deon understands the struggles of the modern diner, said general manager and Chief Justice of the Supreme Court of Casual Dining, Marcus Halloway. Halloway presided over the swearing-in, which involved Deon placing his hand on a laminated dessert menu and promising to uphold the values of the Eatin’ Good in the Neighborhood slogan.

The mayoral duties reportedly include greeting newcomers at the door, mediating disputes over the television remote, and ensuring that the trivia machine remains functional at all times. Political analysts suggest that Deon’s incumbency could last for several years, or at least until his reward points balance reaches a level that allows for total secession from the corporate entity.

Critics have raised concerns regarding Deon’s close ties to the local Buffalo Wild Wings, but the new mayor dismissed these allegations as political noise designed to distract from his plan to subsidize the Triple Hog Dare Ya sandwich.

At press time, Deon was seen vetoing a motion from the waitstaff to close the kitchen ten minutes early.
