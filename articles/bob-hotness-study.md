title: Study Finds People Named Bob Are 100% Hotter Than Everyone Else
date: 2026-10-05
category: Science
author: Dana Whitcomb
dateline: COLUMBUS, OH
summary: Researchers say the results "weren't even close."
---
In a landmark study released Monday, researchers at the Midwest Institute for Attractiveness Science confirmed that people named Bob are exactly 100 percent hotter than everyone else, a finding experts are calling long overdue.

"We controlled for everything," said lead author Dr. Carol Fenwick, who later admitted the team had only studied Bobs. "Height, income, jawline. None of it mattered. The Bob effect simply overwhelmed the data."

The four-year study asked 2,000 participants to rate photos of strangers. Participants were never told anyone's name. The Bobs won anyway.

"We assumed Robert would perform about the same," Fenwick added. "He did not. Robert is a different animal entirely."

## Bobs react

Local Bobs greeted the news with what researchers described as quiet, almost unbearable confidence.

"I've known for years," said Bob Danforth, 54, adjusting his sunglasses indoors. "It's nice that science finally caught up."

> "The Bob effect simply overwhelmed the data."

Not everyone welcomed the findings. A coalition of men named Greg has already demanded a recount, calling the study "rigged from the start" and "honestly kind of hurtful."

At press time, the institute had received more than 14,000 requests for legal name changes, all of them to Bob.
