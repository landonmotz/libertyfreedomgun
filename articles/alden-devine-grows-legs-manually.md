Alden DeVine, a 38-year-old character actor and physical comedian, has baffled the American Medical Association by successfully growing full-sized legs after being born with only small, fleshy protrusions. The performer, known locally for his high-energy stage presence, reportedly achieved the feat through a combination of manual stretching and what he describes as intensive bone-will.

DeVine spent the first three decades of his life navigating the world on what he calls his starter nubs, which measured approximately four inches from the hip. According to researchers at the Des Moines Institute of Bone Density, the actor’s new limbs are not the result of surgery or prosthetic intervention, but rather a slow, painful elongation of his existing biological matter.

## A Victory Of Mind Over Marrow

"We honestly thought he was just standing on a very tall stool for a few months," said Dr. Leonard Vance, a specialist in regenerative orthopedics who has monitored DeVine since 2019. "However, X-rays confirm that Mr. DeVine has somehow forced his femur to sprout from nothingness through what he describes as a combination of positive thinking and deep-tissue pulling. It defies every law of human anatomy we currently hold dear."

> "I just kept pulling on the nubs every morning until I felt a pop, and eventually the shins followed."

The process, which DeVine refers to as the Great Unfolding, reportedly required him to consume upwards of fourteen gallons of whole milk per week to provide the necessary calcium for his rapidly expanding skeleton. Neighbors reported hearing loud, wood-like creaking sounds emanating from DeVine’s apartment during his peak growth spurts in the summer of 2022. While his gait remains somewhat stiff, the actor is now able to buy trousers off the rack for the first time in his life.

At press time, DeVine was reportedly working on extending his toes to a standard human length through a similar series of nightly tension exercises.
