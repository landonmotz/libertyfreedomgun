title: Local Man Confident He Could Land Plane After Watching Half of One YouTube Video
date: 2026-10-04
category: Local
author: Staff Reporter
dateline: TAMPA, FL
summary: "Honestly, how hard can it be?" asked the man, who cannot drive stick.
---
Area resident Kyle Brandt, 31, told friends Saturday that he could "100 percent" land a commercial airliner in an emergency, citing roughly six minutes of a YouTube video he watched in 2021.

"You just pull back on the wheel thing," said Brandt, miming a steering motion that experts say does not exist in any aircraft. "The plane basically wants to land. You just have to let it."

Brandt, who has twice backed into the same mailbox, said the video covered "most of the important stuff" before he clicked on a different video about a raccoon.

> "The plane basically wants to land. You just have to let it."

Sources close to Brandt confirmed he has never been inside a cockpit. They noted, however, that he is "really good" at a flight game he last played on a friend's computer in 2009.

At press time, Brandt was explaining to a group of skeptical coworkers that air traffic control would "talk him through the rest."
