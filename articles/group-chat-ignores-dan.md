A group chat of six longtime friends entered its fourth straight day of not responding to a message from member Dan Kowalski, sources confirmed Thursday.

The message, a two-hour podcast link with the words "you guys HAVE to listen to this," has been seen by every member of the chat, according to read receipts.

"Nobody planned it," said one member, speaking on condition of anonymity because he is also in the chat. "We all just looked at it and thought, someone else will handle this."

The chat has otherwise stayed active. Since the original message, members have shared 41 memes, drafted a fantasy football league, and argued for nearly three hours about whether a hot dog is a sandwich.

## A follow-up

On Wednesday, Kowalski sent a second message reading only "?" which two members immediately liked.

Experts say the situation is not unusual. "Every group chat has a Dan," said Dr. Priya Nandakumar, who studies digital friendship. "Statistically, you may be the Dan. Most Dans never find out."

At press time, Kowalski had sent a third message, another podcast.
