Local accountant Michael Smith underwent a severe and medically unprecedented anaphylactic response to a standard yellowjacket sting Tuesday afternoon, resulting in his complete biological transition into an 11-inch rainbow trout.

According to family members, Smith was clearing brush near the garage when he sustained a single puncture to his left forearm. While his wife, Brenda, rushed to retrieve an over-the-counter antihistamine, Smith reportedly lost footing, shed his cargo shorts, and began flopping rhythmically on the gravel driveway.

Dr. Raymond Vance, chief of immunology at Delaware County Memorial Hospital, stated that while hives, swelling, and lightheadedness are typical reactions, complete freshwater ichthyological reorganization accounts for fewer than 0.0003% of all documented hymenoptera exposures.

"We typically prepare for airway constriction, but in Mr. Smith's case, his respiratory tract reorganized into fully functional gill rakers within ninety seconds," said Vance, checking the salinity of a bedside utility sink. "His vitals are stable, though he possesses no eyelids and his lateral line is extremely sensitive to fluorescent lighting."

Friends and coworkers have attempted to provide support, though communicating with the payroll specialist has proved challenging. Human resources at Smith's accounting firm confirmed that he has been granted two weeks of short-term medical leave, provided he submits weekly water temperature logs to demonstrate ongoing convalescence.

Brenda Smith noted that while the transition has been difficult on the household, she remains optimistic about his prognosis.

"Michael always hated mowing the lawn anyway," Brenda said, sprinkling three dried mealworms into the living room tub. "He just circles the filter now. I think he misses his golf league, but it is hard to tell because his mouth just opens and closes at regular four-second intervals."

At press time, Smith was seen hovering motionless behind an artificial ceramic castle, visibly agitated by a lawnmower running next door.
