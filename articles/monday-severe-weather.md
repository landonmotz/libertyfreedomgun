title: Federal Officials Classify Monday as a Severe Weather Event
date: 2026-10-02
category: Nation
author: Staff Reporter
dateline: WASHINGTON
summary: Residents are urged to stay indoors and avoid eye contact with their inboxes.
---
Citing years of mounting evidence, the Federal Office of Weekdays announced Sunday night that Monday will now be classified as a severe weather event, placing it alongside hurricanes, blizzards, and the second half of February.

"This is not a decision we made lightly," said agency spokesperson Linda Marsh, speaking from beneath a weighted blanket. "But the data is clear. Mondays are causing widespread damage."

According to the agency's report, Mondays account for 78 percent of all spilled coffee, 64 percent of forgotten passwords, and nearly every meeting that could have been an email.

## What residents should do

Officials urged Americans to prepare an emergency kit containing snacks, a phone charger, and at least one excuse that sounds believable.

Marsh added that residents should not attempt to be productive before 10 a.m. "It is simply not safe," she said.

> "Mondays are causing widespread damage."

Several states have already declared a state of emergency, though officials admit this happens every week.

At press time, the agency had issued a tornado watch for Tuesday.
