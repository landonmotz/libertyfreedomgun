Local resident Ben underwent a radical physiological transformation Tuesday afternoon after accidentally ingesting a common garden beetle, resulting in a facial structure described by onlookers as sculpted by the gods. The incident occurred during a backyard gathering when Ben reportedly swallowed a Neon-Striped Stink Bug that had landed on his sandwich. Witnesses state that within three minutes of consumption, Ben’s soft features hardened into a series of razor-sharp angles, his cheekbones rose by four inches, and his chin became a perfectly cleft monument to masculinity.

## A Medical Marvel In The Making

Medical professionals at the Tri-County Aesthetics Institute have struggled to explain the phenomenon. Dr. Leonard Haze, a specialist in spontaneous mutation, noted that the chitinous shell of the beetle may have triggered a dormant hyper-masculinity gene. "The subject now possesses a facial symmetry score of 99.8 percent," Dr. Haze said. "His jawline is capable of slicing thick-cut artisan bread, and his eyes have adopted a soulful, heavy-lidded gaze that suggests a deep familiarity with the works of T.S. Eliot."

> His jawline is capable of slicing thick-cut artisan bread.

Friends of the victim, who formerly described Ben as having a face like a well-meaning potato, expressed both awe and concern. The transformation has reportedly made it difficult for Ben to perform basic tasks, as his intense, brooding expression causes passersby to weep openly in the street. Preliminary reports suggest that his vocal cords have also dropped two octaves, turning his standard greeting into a resonant, symphonic experience.

Despite the physical upgrade, Ben has complained that his new, bulbous head feels significantly heavier than before. He has also expressed a sudden, inexplicable urge to take up the clarinet and move into a hollowed-out Easter Island statue. 

At press time, Ben was seen staring intensely into a puddle, unsure if he was looking at himself or a masterpiece of Renaissance sculpture.
